About Us

Team 5 is a group of students dedicated to developing innovative and practical software solutions through the integration of modern technologies and intelligent systems. Our project focuses on creating a Posture Analysis System that leverages Machine Learning and Convolutional Neural Networks (CNN) to analyze user posture from uploaded images.

The goal of this project is to help individuals become more aware of their posture habits by providing accurate posture assessments, personalized ergonomic feedback, and improvement recommendations. By storing posture analysis results in a database, the system also allows users to track their posture history and monitor their progress over time.

Through this project, Team 5 aims to demonstrate how software engineering, machine learning, and data analysis can be combined to develop a practical health-oriented application that promotes better posture and overall well-being.
